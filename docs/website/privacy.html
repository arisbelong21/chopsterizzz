Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Chopster desktop application ("the Software") processes, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have. The Software is provided by GANTI_NAMA_PENGEMBANG ("we", "us").

1. Summary

Chopster runs entirely on your own computer. We operate no server and we do not receive, collect, or store your content, tokens, or personal data. Everything the Software needs in order to work stays on your device.

2. Information the Software processes

CategoryExamplesWhy
TikTok authorisation data Access token, refresh token, open ID, token expiry So the Software can publish to the TikTok account that you explicitly authorised
TikTok profile data Display name and basic profile information Only to show you which account is connected
Your content Video files, captions, titles that you select or write To prepare and upload that content on your instruction
Local application data Settings, job history, log files So the Software works and so you can review what it did

The Software does not collect your TikTok password. Sign-in happens on TikTok's own website, and the Software only receives an access token that you approve.

3. Where information is stored

All of the above is stored locally on your device, in your Windows user profile (for example under %APPDATA%\ChopsterByAris). We do not transmit it to us or to any third party, because we do not operate any server or cloud service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only outbound communication the Software performs is the direct connection between your device and the platform you authorised (for example TikTok's API endpoints), which is required to carry out the publishing action you requested.

5. Retention and deletion

  • You can disconnect a connected account at any time inside the Software. Doing so deletes the stored tokens and the associated local session data immediately.
  • You can also revoke access from your TikTok account settings at any time.
  • Uninstalling the Software and deleting its local data folder removes all remaining data from your device.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children

The Software is not intended for use by children under the age required to hold a TikTok account in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly process data from such children.

7. Security

Data is protected by the security of your own device and your Windows user account. You are responsible for keeping your device and account credentials secure. We recommend using a device you trust and keeping your operating system updated.

8. Analytics and tracking

The Software contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or telemetry.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
